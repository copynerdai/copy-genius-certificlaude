# Research — index

> This folder holds the brand's research documents — market, audience, and competitor research plus the unified research brief. It is populated by the [deep-research](../../../skills/deep-research.md) skill; the copywriter does not author these files manually.

## How this folder is used

- **Written by**: `deep-research` (market / audience / competitor docs + unified brief).
- **Read by**: the strategic frameworks [awareness-levels](../../../core/strategic-frameworks/awareness-levels.md), [mass-desire](../../../core/strategic-frameworks/mass-desire.md), [unique-mechanism](../../../core/strategic-frameworks/unique-mechanism.md), and [proof-elements](../../../core/strategic-frameworks/proof-elements.md) during the Strategist's funnel-brief work.
- **If empty when a funnel brief is being built**: the Strategist flags the research gap to the copywriter and falls back on the avatar files (e.g. [avatar](../avatars/avatar.md)) and the competitor profiles in `competitors/` as inputs.
