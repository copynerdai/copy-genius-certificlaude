# Strategy Notebook

> Your strategy notebook. Drop here notes from courses, podcasts, conversations, books, or anything that triggered an interesting marketing or copywriting thought. Copy Genius reads each entry and distills it through what the wiki already knows: awareness levels, chain of beliefs, persuasion techniques, mechanism construction, offer architecture, funnel logic, and the rest of the strategic framework catalog.
>
> No template. No pre-set categories. Copy Genius analyzes each entry on its own merits and decides what's worth keeping, what connects to existing wiki knowledge, and what might extend or challenge it.

## How to use it

1. **Drop a raw observation** in the Inbox below. Any format: a paragraph of notes, a quote, a screenshot reference, a transcript snippet, a question you couldn't answer.
2. **Ask Copy Genius**: *"distill the latest entry in my strategy notebook"*.
3. **Copy Genius reads the entry** and produces an analysis that:
   - Identifies what the entry is actually saying (the underlying principle, technique, or claim)
   - Cross-references against existing wiki knowledge — confirms, extends, or contradicts
   - Surfaces practical applications (where this would change how you write, structure, or evaluate copy)
   - Flags weak spots (unsupported claims, contextual limits, traps)
   - Suggests where in the wiki this insight could integrate, if anywhere
4. **The distilled entry** is then filed below the Inbox in the Distilled section. Copy Genius organizes the Distilled section organically — no pre-imposed taxonomy.

If a distilled entry deserves to become permanent wiki knowledge (a new core concept, a new specialist rule, a new technique), Copy Genius surfaces that as an explicit recommendation. You decide whether to promote it.
