## Social Mirror — How Others See Them

> What people in their immediate circle say and think about them. NOT what they say about themselves (that's Identity) — what OTHERS see and voice. A powerful purchase driver: people often buy as much to shift how their inner circle perceives them as to change how they perceive themselves.

> Capture BOTH what's said openly (verbatim) AND what's perceived but unspoken (what they sense the people around them think but won't say).
